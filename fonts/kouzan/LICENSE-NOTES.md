# 衡山毛筆フォント / Kouzan Brush

Calligrapher: 青柳衡山 (Aoyagi Kouzan). Original font: Version 1.1.
Official source and usage terms: https://opentype.jp/kouzanmouhitufont.htm
Downloaded 2026-10-10 from https://opentype.jp/bin/KouzanMouhituFont.zip.

The publisher states that use is free, commercial use is unrestricted, logos are allowed, and inclusion in magazines, books, and CDs is permitted. No warranty is provided. This font is not licensed under SIL OFL. The page does not separately enumerate web embedding, format conversion, or subsetting; this distribution relies on the broad published free-use and redistribution permission. Original OS/2 fsType is 0x0008 (editable embedding), with no no-subsetting flag.

These WOFF2 files preserve the original character outlines. The malformed legacy gasp table and obsolete mort table were removed for conversion. FontTools generated Unicode-range subsets; coverage excludes missing and blank glyphs, including 綻, 詓, 餺, 鰙. Source checksum and chunk information are recorded in coverage.json. Regenerate with scripts/build_kouzan_font.py (fonttools and brotli required).

This is modern Japanese calligraphy, not a historical inscription or manuscript specimen.
